बोलिए

Tap the mic to try a voice deposit, the way she would.

Three barriers keep her out of formal savings. Unnati is designed around each one.

Literacy, trust and access. Every feature answers one of them.

Literacy

She shouldn't need to read or type to save.

Voice-first, vernacular UI

She speaks to the app in her own language. No complex text to read or type.

Goal-based savings jars

Progress toward goals like "Daughter's education" is shown visually, so saving feels meaningful.

Trust

Money moves when someone she knows stands behind it.

Assisted onboarding

A trusted local Sakhi agent sets up instant, paperless eKYC, building confidence from day one.

Digital gold

A culturally understood, trusted asset that builds on the mental models she already has.

Access

Her income is irregular and her signal is patchy.

Flexible micro-savings

UPI AutoPay sets aside small daily or weekly amounts that fit irregular income.

Offline-first architecture

The app keeps working on unreliable internet, so the experience stays consistent and dependable.

For her, the agent is the platform.

Our go-to-market wraps the digital product in a human firewall of trust. Each layer is a person or a place she already knows.

  • Strategic partnerships

    State Rural Livelihood Missions (SRLMs) and local NGOs open the door to established SHG networks.

  • The Sakhi agent network

    Respected women from local SHGs, trained as Unnati's human interface, and earning as micro-entrepreneurs.

  • Last-mile Unnati Points

    Local kirana stores handle cash deposits and withdrawals, forming a micro-ATM network in every village.

Our vision

To be the most trusted and accessible financial companion for every rural woman in India.

We want her to save with confidence, grow her wealth securely, and reach the aspirations she holds for her family.

The roadmap to impact

A phased path from a trusted savings habit to a complete financial wellness ecosystem.

  1. 1

    Q1 to Q2

    Foundation & trust

    Validation

    • Validate the core value proposition in Dharashiv district.
    • Test and refine the Sakhi agent model.
    • Launch all MVP features and reach product-market fit.
  2. 2

    Q3 to Q4

    Engagement & diversification

    Habit and education

    • Deepen engagement with gamified financial literacy.
    • Launch the Sakhi Agent Dashboard to empower the network.
    • Add Recurring Deposits through Small Finance Bank partnerships.
  3. 3

    Year 2

    Ecosystem expansion

    Resilience and growth

    • Grow into a holistic financial wellness platform.
    • Introduce micro-insurance and micro-credit.
    • Launch the SHG Leader Dashboard to strengthen communities.
