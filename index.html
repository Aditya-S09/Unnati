Empowering Rural Women

Aapke Sapne, ab aapki muthhi mein!

Unnati is a 'phygital' financial platform designed to unlock the largest untapped savings market in India: rural women. We build trust and drive adoption through a simple, voice-operated app and a network of local agents.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and accessible financial companion for every rural woman in India. We aim to empower her to save confidently, securely grow her wealth and ultimately achieve her family's aspirations.

A Radically Simple Solution

We address the core barriers of literacy, trust and accessibility with features designed specifically for our users.

Voice-First, Vernacular UI

Users can interact with the app in their own language, by speaking. No need to read or type complex text.

Assisted Onboarding

Trusted local 'Sakhi' agents help with instant, paperless eKYC setup, building confidence from day one.

Goal-Based Savings Jars

Visually track progress towards tangible, emotional goals like "Daughter's Education," making saving meaningful.

Flexible Micro-Savings

UPI AutoPay allows for small, automated daily or weekly savings, catering to irregular income streams.

Digital Gold Investment

A culturally understood and trusted asset class that simplifies the value proposition and builds on existing mental models.

Offline-First Architecture

The app remains functional even with unreliable internet, ensuring a trustworthy and consistent experience.

Our 'Phygital' Go-to-Market Strategy

For our users, the agent is the platform. Our success is built on a human firewall of trust and support around the digital product.

Strategic Partnerships

We partner with State Rural Livelihood Missions (SRLMs) and local NGOs to gain access to established SHG networks.

The 'Sakhi' Agent Network

We recruit and train respected women from local SHGs to act as the human interface for Unnati, turning them into micro-entrepreneurs.

Last-Mile Infrastructure

Local Kirana stores act as 'Unnati Points' for cash deposits and withdrawals, creating a robust micro-ATM network.

Our Roadmap to Impact

A phased approach to building a holistic financial wellness ecosystem.

Phase 1: Foundation & Trust

Q1-Q2: Validation

  • Validate core value proposition in Dharashiv district.
  • Test and refine the "Sakhi" agent model.
  • Launch all MVP features to achieve product-market fit.

Phase 2: Engagement & Diversification

Q3-Q4: Habit & Education

  • Deepen user engagement with gamified financial literacy.
  • Launch "Sakhi" Agent Dashboard to empower our network.
  • Expand product offerings with Recurring Deposits via SFB partnerships.

Phase 3: Ecosystem Expansion

Year 2: Resilience & Growth

  • Evolve into a holistic wellness platform.
  • Introduce micro-insurance and micro-credit products.
  • Launch SHG Leader Dashboard to empower communities.
